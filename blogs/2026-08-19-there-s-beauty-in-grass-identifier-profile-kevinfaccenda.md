Identifier Profile · Kevin Faccenda ( @kevinfaccenda ) Photo of Kevin by Jimmy Parker In 2021, Kevin Faccenda ( @kevinfaccenda ) started walking the University of Hawai’i campus, collecting every grass he could find. Right outside the botany building, he found one that had never been recorded in Hawai’i ( Urochloa glumaris ), and he’s been working on grass taxonomy ever since. Kevin studied invasive grasses for his PhD at the University of Hawai’i at Mānoa, and his main identification focus on iNaturalist is grasses of the Pacific.
