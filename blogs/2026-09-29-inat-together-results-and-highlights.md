Exploring nature is better together iNat Together: Find Your Nature Friends ran from September 18 through 28, and we asked one thing of everyone: bring someone. You did! iNaturalist Ambassadors hosted more than 280 events, from campus bioblitzes to blacklight moth nights, and people with no event nearby made plans with a friend and joined the open project .
