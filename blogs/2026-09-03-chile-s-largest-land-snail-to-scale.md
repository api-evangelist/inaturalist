Behind the Observation · Macrocyclis peruvianus The Chilean Black Snail ( Macrocyclis peruvianus ) is the only living species in its genus, and the largest land snail in Chile. It’s also a snail that eats other snails, along with mushrooms. Pablo Silva Reyes ( @sur_endemico ) photographed this one in Parque Nacional Alerce Costero, in the Valdivian rainforest of southern Chile.
