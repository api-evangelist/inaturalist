Identifier Profile · Ariel Bonkoski ( @arielbonkoski ) Photo of Ariel by Brooks Bonkoski Ariel Bonkoski ( @arielbonkoski ) is based in Duluth, Minnesota, where she teaches mushroom foraging and identification. She is the author of Mushrooms & Fungi For Kids and is also Co-President of the Head of the Lakes Chapter of the Minnesota Master Naturalist program. On iNaturalist, she identifies fungi, especially polypores.
