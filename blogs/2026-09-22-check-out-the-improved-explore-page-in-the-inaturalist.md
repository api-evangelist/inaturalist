The Explore page in iNaturalist is where you can go to see everything that's been observed on iNaturalist. You can find the species right near you, observations in places you love, recent sightings at future travel destinations, and see the fellow iNaturalist community members that made those observations. This has historically been easiest to do on the iNaturalist website, but now, the iPhone app has a new and improved update to the Explore page that makes it easier to browse observations even when you're away from a computer!
